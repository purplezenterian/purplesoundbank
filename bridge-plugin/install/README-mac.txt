PSB BRIDGE (Mac)

1. Double-click "Install-PSB-Bridge.command". If your Mac blocks it, right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
2. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins (Logic does it by itself).
3. Add "PSB Bridge" as an instrument on a track (it is listed under Purple SoundBank).
4. Open the Purple SoundBank hub in Chrome or Edge, start the instrument, press the DAW button at the top right, then Connect to plugin.

Plays through any DAW that takes VST3 or Audio Units (Logic, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig and more).
